    How to play

    A new athlete every day, and a separate daily game for each sport. Pick one from the bar at the top. You get 10 guesses.

    After each guess, every box shows how close you were:

    • Club / Team / League: green if it matches.
    • Nation: green if it matches, yellow if same continent.
    • Position: green if it matches. In the NFL, yellow means same side of the ball.
    • Age: yellow if within 3 years. Arrows point toward the answer.
    • Height: yellow if within 2 inches.
    • Net worth: yellow if within 25%.

    Stuck? After 4 guesses, pick 1 of 3 hint cards. After 8 guesses, pick one of the other two.

    Teams are from the 2025/26 season. Net worth figures are rough public estimates. Photos and logos come from Wikipedia and TheSportsDB.
